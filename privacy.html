About this policy

Tanner Bair operates Tank Studio under TheAvgDevs. This policy describes the current freshwater planning and care app, including its TestFlight beta. For privacy questions or requests, email TheAvgDevs@gmail.com.

Your tank records

Tank names, plans, livestock, plants, equipment, appearance choices, routines, readings, care history, and journal notes and photos are saved in the app’s private storage on your device. Tank Studio does not send these records to a developer-operated server. No account is required. The app does not add a separate password or encryption layer for these records; use your device’s security settings and avoid storing secrets in notes.

The app does not include advertising, tracking, third-party analytics SDKs, location access, or an AI service that receives your records. We do not sell your personal information or use it for targeted advertising.

Photos you choose

The system photo picker gives Tank Studio only the photos you select. It does not give the app access to your whole library. Apple may need to download a selected photo from iCloud Photos. Tank Studio prepares a smaller JPEG copy for a journal entry. It does not modify or delete the original in Photos.

Journal images are not analyzed to identify animals, read test results, or diagnose problems. Photo contents may themselves contain personal information, so choose what you save carefully.

Reminders

Phone reminders are optional. If you enable them, iOS stores local notification requests based on your routines. Notifications can show your routine title, tank name, and a message that planned care is due on your lock screen, depending on your system settings. You can disable reminders in the app or change notification permissions and previews in Settings. No push-notification server is used.

Sharing and outside links

Tank plan cards are shared only when you choose to use the system share sheet. Review the preview before sharing. A card can include the tank name and the plan details you select. Cards exclude care routines, readings, history, journal notes and photos, and equipment manufacturer, model, and notes. The receiving app or person controls any copy you send.

Opening a cited source, Apple information page, or email link takes you to an outside service. That service may receive ordinary connection information and follows its own privacy policy. Tank Studio does not attach your saved tank records to those links.

Some equipment and supply screens may show Amazon links after the app shows what your tank needs. As an Amazon Associate, Tank Studio earns from qualifying purchases. These links open in Safari, add only an Associates tag, and never include your tank records. Livestock never has shopping links. Amazon's own privacy policy applies once you leave the app.

Device backups and deletion

The app does not provide its own iCloud sync, account recovery, or general data export. iOS or computer backups may include app data depending on your settings. Backups are separate from app sync. Restoring a particular tank or photo is not guaranteed by Tank Studio.

You can edit or delete journal entries and remove their attached copies in the app. Removing an attachment does not delete the original in Photos. Archiving a tank preserves its records; it is not deletion. Managed tanks and immutable care history do not have an individual permanent-delete control in this beta.

To protect photos when saved data is damaged, automatic cleanup of private photo copies without matching journal records is temporarily paused. Unused copies, including copies left after a failed removal, may remain on your device indefinitely and use storage. Deleting an entry or removing or replacing its attachment still attempts to remove that specific copy. This safeguard does not restore missing records or photos.

Deleting Tank Studio from your device removes its local app data; offloading the app preserves data. Do not delete the app to troubleshoot a saved-data error. Copies in device backups, Photos, or apps you shared to must be managed separately through those services. We cannot retrieve or remotely erase records held only on your device.

TestFlight and support

When you use TestFlight, Apple automatically collects beta-testing activity and crash information and shares it with the developer. Apple also shares feedback you submit. We use this information to investigate issues and improve Tank Studio. We do not share TestFlight information with third parties. Feedback may include screenshots, comments, contact information, and device or app details. Public-link invitations do not reveal your name or email to us unless you provide contact information with feedback. This is separate from Tank Studio’s local tank storage and is governed by Apple’s TestFlight terms and privacy information.

If you email support or submit feedback, we receive what you choose to send and use it to answer you, investigate problems, and improve the app. Email is handled through Gmail. Avoid sending private journal material unless you want it included in your request. We do not automatically attach tank records, photos, or logs to support emails.

We retain support correspondence and feedback only as needed to handle the request, investigate related issues, and meet applicable obligations. You can request deletion of developer-held correspondence at TheAvgDevs@gmail.com. Information retained independently by Apple, Google, or a recipient is subject to that service’s controls and policies.

Policy updates

We will update this policy when the app’s practices change and show the revised date. Any future account, cloud, analytics, or commerce feature will need an updated disclosure before it is introduced.
