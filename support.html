Contact us

Email TheAvgDevs@gmail.com for help, privacy requests, or corrections to a source or species profile. TestFlight users can also open TestFlight, select Tank Studio, and choose Send Beta Feedback.

For a bug report, include the app version and build, device model, iOS version, what you tried, what you expected, and what happened. A screenshot is optional; check it for personal information before sending. Never send passwords or Apple Account credentials.

If a save or photo fails

Keep the app installed. Close and reopen it, check available device storage, and note the exact message. If saved tanks cannot be opened, do not delete the app or replace your data to get past the recovery screen; contact support first. We cannot recover a local record that has been deleted.

For a photo that will not import, try a smaller image and confirm that the selected image is available in Photos. A photo held only in iCloud Photos may need a connection before it can be selected successfully.

If a reminder does not appear

Check that reminders are enabled for your routines, then check Tank Studio’s notification permission, Focus settings, and notification previews in iOS Settings. An archived tank pauses reminder input. Notification delivery never completes a routine; log the action after you do it.

What to try in this beta

Create a draft, preview a livestock or habitat change, cancel one preview, and confirm another. Switch between drafts and check that each keeps its own records. If you choose to manage a tank, add a routine, record a water test, and save a journal note or photo. Close and reopen the app to check that your changes remain.

Tell us where you got stuck or where a finding was hard to understand. Use test records for unfamiliar flows and keep another record of care information you cannot afford to lose during beta testing.

Questions or corrections? TheAvgDevs@gmail.com
