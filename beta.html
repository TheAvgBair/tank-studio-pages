A freshwater planning aid

Tank Studio helps you organize a freshwater aquarium plan and your own care records. This beta may contain errors or change between builds. Illustrations are decorative and do not measure usable swimming space, water volume, or habitat.

Read the findings and their limits

Conflict identifies a documented incompatibility or material shortfall. Caution identifies a documented risk or a smaller shortfall. Unknown means the app does not have enough reviewed information to make that assessment. Read the reason, proposed changes, and sources before acting.

No Conflict does not establish that a tank is safe or fully compatible. Combined stocking capacity, swimming footprint, pH and hardness suitability, oxygen, maturity, sex ratios, and many animal interactions are not fully evaluated. A source-checked profile is not independent expert approval. Verify the exact species, life stage, and setup context before buying animals or changing their care.

Water checks and care records

Water checks interpret recorded ammonia and nitrite using a limited dated policy. They do not certify that an aquarium is cycled or safe. Nitrate, pH, GH, KH, and temperature readings remain records without matching assessment rules. Blank measurements stay unknown.

The app does not diagnose disease, interpret photos, or calculate medication or treatment doses. Starting to manage a tank changes how the app organizes records. It does not approve adding animals. Saving a change updates your plan or recorded inventory, not the physical aquarium. Consult appropriate qualified help for an animal-health concern.

Local records during the beta

This build supports separate drafts and one managed tank. There is no app account, app-managed cloud sync, or general data export. Keep a separate record of essential care information. Read the privacy policy for device backups, sharing, and deletion details.
